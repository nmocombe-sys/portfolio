Communications Experience

Bowdoin College

In 2023 was personally selected by the Dean of Students at Bowdoin to work with the College’s Communications Office as a multimedia correspondent. Since then, I’ve met weekly with the President and SVP of Communications to ensure consistent messaging between students, staff, faculty, and the border community. I’ve grown as a communicator, editor, and storyteller. I’m proud to say that the content I’ve produced has been the most engaged on the college’s social media channels to date. If you’d like to learn more about my work, I’ve left a brief sample of my experience here.

AgentryPR

I worked with AgentryPR as a summer intern in the fashion marketing and business development division. In my role, I aided in the ideation of marketing campaigns for brands including but not limited to Cadillac, Mclaren, Onitsuka Tiger, Abercrombie and Fitch, Diesel, and Uniqlo. On the fashion side, I packed, pulled, and delivered couture pieces to celebrities like Ariana Grande, Lady Gaga, Flo Milli, Sabrina Carpenter, Jannet Jackson, Kim Kardashian.

GriffinPR

Since 2024, I have worked closely with Sara Griffin at GriffinPR as an architecture and design intern. I proactively handled administrative and logistical tasks around the office, in addition to creating a database for associates at the firm to quickly identify relevant writers to cover client events. Clients include but not limited to the Smithsonian Museum, Google, Brooklyn Botanic, Virta, AM Collective, Caline Chagoury Moudaber.

The Moth

I’m currently working as a Production Intern for The Moth, a global storytelling organization headquartered in New York City. I handle daily administrative tasks, contribute to the ideation for upcoming StorySLAMs and coordinate with regional producers around the world in order to make the power of storytelling more democratic. I’m grateful to have worked with clients/venues including but not limited to: the Sydney Opera House, Malcolm Gladwell, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation.
